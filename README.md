## 🌟 Featured Engineering Projects

> ### 📄 **[Intelligent Document Q&A System (RAG)](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/rag-document-qa)**
> 
> * **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `LangChain (LCEL)` • `ChromaDB` • `Google Gemini` • `Streamlit` • `Sentence-Transformers`
> * **Architecture:** End-to-end Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) web app processing multi-page documents via recursive text chunking and vector indexing. Enforces strict zero-hallucination prompt constraints with exact page-level source citations.
> * **Features:** Dynamic context-depth sliding, session caching, and live cloud deployment.

---

> ### 🍕 **[Zomato Live Operations & Fulfillment Analytics Suite](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/zomato-operations-analytics-powerbi)**
> 
> * **Tech Stack:** `Power BI` • `DAX` • `Data Modeling` • `Excel` • `Business Intelligence`
> * **Architecture:** Operational business intelligence dashboard evaluating multi-city food delivery unit economics, Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), SLA delivery delays, payment channel splits, and customer satisfaction metrics.

---

> ### 🛡️ **[FinTech Digital Payments & Transaction Risk Analytics](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/DD-fintech-transaction-risk-analytics)**
> 
> * **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `Streamlit` • `Plotly` • `Scikit-learn` • `SQL`
> * **Architecture:** Payment anomaly detection engine evaluating 1,000+ high-frequency records (₹10.2L+ GTV) using supervised Random Forest classification to detect and isolate ₹3.4L+ in high-risk fraud exposure.

---

> ### 🏏 **[IPL AI Analytics Hub](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/ipl-ai-analytics-hub)**
> 
> * **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `Flask` • `Machine Learning` • `EDA`
> * **Architecture:** Sports analytics platform featuring live match state simulation engines, dynamic ball-by-ball win-probability calculators, and player scouting auction valuations.

---

> ### 🎓 **[Enterprise Placement Analytics AI](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/DD-_-placement-analytics-ai)**
> 
> * **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `Streamlit` • `Plotly Express` • `Statistical Analysis`
> * **Architecture:** Recruitment insights platform mapping campus compensation trajectories, placement conversion percentages, and department-specific hiring velocities.

---

> ### 🗣️ **[indicVoice — Multilingual Speech & NLP Translation Hub](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/indicVoice)**
> 
> * **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `Flask` • `NLP APIs` • `Speech Recognition` • `JavaScript`
> * **Architecture:** Real-time audio processing pipeline handling automatic speech recognition (ASR) and cross-lingual translation across regional Indian dialects.

---

> ### 🤖 **[-DD-telegram-bot-](https://github.com/DebarkDutta-24/-DD-telegram-bot-)**
